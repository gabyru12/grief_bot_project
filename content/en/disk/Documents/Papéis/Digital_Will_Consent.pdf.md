If something happens to me, this is what I want done with my digital life.

## What may be kept

- **Photos.** My family and friends may keep my photos as a memory.

## What must be deleted

- My messages and chat history.
- My diary and personal documents.
- My voice notes and videos.

## What I do not allow

I do **not** allow anyone to use my messages, photos, voice or writing to create an AI, a chatbot or any simulation of me: not of my voice, not of my image, not of my personality. Keeping my files is not permission to train anything on them.

> I'd rather be remembered than imitated.
